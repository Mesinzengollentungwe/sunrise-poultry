Our Story

Quality chicken, healthy life one honest batch at a time.

Sun Rise Poultry Enterprise is a broiler production farm based in Nomayos, on the outskirts of Yaoundé. We raise day-old chicks through a disciplined 45-day cycle brooding, growing and finishing under strict vaccination and biosecurity protocols, so every bird that leaves our farm gate is healthy, traceable and genuinely fresh.

We sell live birds directly to households, restaurants, hotels, caterers and market agents across Yaoundé, with pickup at the farm or scheduled motorbike delivery. No middlemen, no frozen imports just a farm you can call, visit, and trust.

✓

Strict Biosecurity

Disinfection, quarantine and a vaccination schedule at every stage of the cycle.

⏱

Never Held Past 60 Days

A firm ceiling on rearing time keeps birds healthy and feed costs in check.

Relationship-First Selling

From Chick to Farm Gate

How every batch is raised

Every bird follows the same disciplined, all-in-all-out cycle so quality never depends on luck.

1
Day 0

Pre-Arrival Prep

Full clean-out, disinfection and brooder pre-heating before chicks arrive.

2
Day 1–14

Brooding

Day-old chicks kept warm with continuous starter feed and clean water.

3
Day 15–35

Growing

Grower feed, more space, and Newcastle & Gumboro vaccinations on schedule.

4
Day 36–45

Finishing

Finisher feed to reach healthy market weight, closely monitored for feed conversion.

5
Day 45+

Sale & Delivery

Sold live at the farm gate or delivered fresh never held past day 60.
