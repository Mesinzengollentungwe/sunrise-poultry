Quality, Freshness, Trust

A small farm run with a big-farm's discipline.

We're a start-up broiler operation based in Nomayos, on the outskirts of Yaoundé — close enough for same-day delivery, far enough from the city to keep our biosecurity tight. Every cycle starts with certified day-old chicks, a strict vaccination schedule, and daily record-keeping of feed, weight and mortality, so you always know exactly what you're buying.

Day 1 to day 45

How every batch is raised

Each batch of 500 chicks moves through the same disciplined, all-in-all-out cycle — so the bird you order this month is raised exactly the same way as the one you ordered last month.

Day 1 – 14
1

Brooding

Day-old chicks kept warm in a heated brooder with continuous starter feed and clean water.

Day 15 – 35
2

Growing

Moved onto grower feed with more floor space; Newcastle and Gumboro vaccinations administered.

Day 36 – 45
3

Finishing

Finisher feed maximizes healthy weight gain ahead of the standard 45-day market point.

Day 45+
4

Market day

Sold live at 3,500 FCFA. Any bird held to day 50–55 for extra size sells at a 5,000 FCFA premium — never past day 60.
